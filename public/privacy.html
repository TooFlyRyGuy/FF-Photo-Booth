Privacy Policy

Last Updated: January 22, 2025

Fun Frame Photo ("we," "us," or "our") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our AI-powered photo booth services.

Key Commitment: We Do Not Sell Your Personal Information

We do not sell your personal information to third parties, nor do we share it for monetary or other valuable consideration.

1. Information We Collect

1.1 Information You Provide to Us

  • Account Information: Email address, password, name, company name, phone number
  • Profile Information: Display name, profile settings, preferences
  • Payment Information: Processed securely through Stripe (we do not store full credit card numbers)
  • Event Data: Event names, dates, settings, configurations
  • Content: Photos you upload, AI image generation prompts, generated images
  • Communications: Support messages, feedback, and correspondence

1.2 Information Collected Automatically

  • Usage Data: Features used, events created, images generated, credits consumed
  • Device Information: IP address, browser type, operating system
  • Log Data: Access times, pages viewed, errors encountered
  • Analytics: How you interact with our service (via cookies and similar technologies)

1.3 Information from Third Parties

  • Authentication Providers: If you use social login (if available)
  • Payment Processors: Stripe payment confirmations and subscription status
  • Cloud Storage: Dropbox and SmugMug authentication tokens (stored securely)

2. Event Host vs. Event Guest Data

2.1 Fun Frame Photo's Role

For Event Host Data: We are the data controller. We determine how Event Host account data is collected and used.

For Event Guest Data: We are a service provider/data processor. Event Hosts control how they collect and use Event Guest data.

2.2 Event Guest Data

When Event Guests participate in events via kiosk mode, the following data may be collected:

  • Phone numbers (if SMS delivery is used)
  • Photos taken during the event
  • AI-generated images
  • Event participation timestamps
  • Device identifiers (browser token and IP address) when the event organizer has enabled the per-device photo limit feature, used solely to enforce that limit

2.3 Event Host Responsibilities

IMPORTANT: Event Hosts are responsible for:

  • Obtaining consent from Event Guests to collect their data
  • Providing Event Guests with appropriate privacy notices
  • Complying with all applicable privacy laws (GDPR, CCPA, etc.)
  • Handling Event Guest requests (access, deletion, opt-out)
  • Securing any data they download or access

Fun Frame Photo is not responsible for Event Hosts' data practices. If you are an Event Guest with concerns about how your data is used, please contact the Event Host directly.

  • Analyze usage patterns and trends
  • Debug and fix technical issues
  • Develop new features and functionality
  • Conduct research and testing

3.3 To Communicate With You

  • Send service-related notifications
  • Respond to your inquiries
  • Send marketing communications (with your consent)
  • Notify you of changes to our Terms or Privacy Policy

3.4 For Security and Compliance

  • Prevent fraud and abuse
  • Enforce our Terms of Use
  • Comply with legal obligations
  • Protect our rights and property

4.1 We Do Not Sell Your Information

We do not sell your personal information to third parties. We do not share your information for monetary or other valuable consideration.

We may disclose information if required by law, subpoena, or other legal process, or to protect our rights, property, or safety.

We may share information with third parties when you explicitly consent to such sharing.

5. Data Retention

  • Account Data: Retained while your account is active and for a reasonable period after deletion
  • Generated Images: Retained according to your storage settings and subscription plan
  • Usage Data: Retained for up to 2 years for analytics purposes
  • Device Usage Data: When per-device photo limiting is enabled, device identifiers (browser token and IP address) are retained for up to 6 months after the device's last interaction, then automatically deleted
  • Payment Records: Retained for 7 years to comply with tax and financial regulations

You may request deletion of your data at any time by contacting us or using account settings.

6. Data Security

We implement industry-standard security measures to protect your information:

  • Encryption in transit (HTTPS/TLS)
  • Encryption at rest for sensitive data
  • Access controls and authentication
  • Regular security audits and monitoring
  • Secure third-party service providers

However, no system is 100% secure. We cannot guarantee absolute security of your information.

8. California Privacy Rights (CCPA)

If you are a California resident, you have additional rights under the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA):

8.1 Right to Know

You have the right to request information about:

  • Categories of personal information we collect
  • Categories of sources from which we collect information
  • Business or commercial purposes for collecting information
  • Categories of third parties with whom we share information
  • Specific pieces of personal information we have collected

8.2 Right to Delete

You have the right to request deletion of your personal information, subject to certain exceptions.

8.3 Right to Opt-Out of Sale

We do not sell your personal information. This right does not apply because we do not engage in data sales.

8.4 Right to Non-Discrimination

We will not discriminate against you for exercising your CCPA rights.

8.5 How to Exercise Your Rights

To exercise your CCPA rights, contact us at:

  • Email: privacy@funframephoto.com
  • Subject line: "CCPA Request"

We will verify your identity and respond within 45 days.

8.6 Authorized Agents

You may designate an authorized agent to make requests on your behalf. The agent must provide proof of authorization.

8.7 California Shine the Light Law

California Civil Code Section 1798.83 permits California residents to request information about disclosure of personal information to third parties for direct marketing purposes. We do not share information for such purposes.
